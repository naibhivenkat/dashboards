Grocery App

Grocery App is an online grocery ordering platform that allows customers to purchase groceries from nearby local shops using a mobile application.

Services Offered

  • Online grocery ordering from local shops
  • Home delivery of grocery items
  • Order tracking
  • Online payments

Products

  • Rice, Wheat, Pulses
  • Vegetables and Fruits
  • Dairy products
  • Packaged food items

Pricing (INR)

Product prices vary based on shop and item selection.

  • Minimum order value: ₹100
  • Delivery charges: ₹20 – ₹50 (based on distance)
  • No platform fee for customers

Business Model

Customers place grocery orders through the mobile application. Payments are collected online. Local shop owners fulfill and deliver the orders. The platform facilitates the transaction between customers and shop owners.

Payment Method

Online payments are accepted via UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card and Net Banking.
